Frequently Asked Questions
Which versions of Odoo are supported?

Our module supports Odoo Enterprise (On-premise and Odoo.sh) as well as Odoo Community.
OdooTranslate is fully usable with Odoo Online, but the module cannot be installed.
Installing the module on Odoo Online is not supported. Learn more on our website.

Do I need a subscription?

You can use our app and all their features free of charge and translate up to 2,000 words per month (in one language).

Beyond this limit, you will need to subscribe to a plan based on the number of words you translate each month.

How many words do I need?

Our app calculates the number of words based on the content (fields) and the number of languages you select.

To access this cost simulation tool, you will need to create an account on our website and connect your Odoo instance.

Is all content translatable?

Yes! We have designed our tools to adapt their functionality to any type of content (messages, emails, chats, pages, articles, etc.).

Can you help me to install & configure OdooTranslate?

If you need help installing and configuring OdooTranslate on your Odoo, please contact us!
